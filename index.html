In a rapidly evolving market, resilient supply chains don’t just react—they stay ahead. I work at the intersection of business strategy and technical execution to help organizations build smarter, more adaptive operations. With a strong foundation in data, systems, and process design, I focus on transforming complexity into clarity—enabling faster decisions, greater transparency, and measurable impact across the value chain.

Throughout my time at IBM, I led and supported several cross-functional projects focused on SAP integration, data modeling, and financial planning automation. My role bridged both technical and functional domains—transforming complex requirements into scalable, insight-driven solutions that improved operational visibility and business alignment.

I’m also deeply passionate about forecasting and data modeling. I enjoy uncovering key drivers that influence forecast accuracy, and iterating through multiple rounds of validation, calibration, and adjustment to arrive at optimal solutions. To me, forecasting is more than just a statistical process—it’s a strategic art that requires business context, technical precision, and continuous learning.

I pursued and passed four Microsoft Azure Fundamentals certifications(AZ-900, DP-900, AI-900, SC-900) out of a deep interest in emerging cloud technologies. I believe cloud platforms, AI, and advanced analytics are critical enablers for greater supply chain transparency, resilience, and innovation.

What draws me to supply chain management is its foundational impact. It’s one of the few domains where technical improvements lead directly to tangible business results—better service levels, smarter inventory, and stronger collaboration across the network. I’m passionate about making that change happen—and making sure it keeps happening.
